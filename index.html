Lab · Design automation with AI

Show AI a reference, not a long prompt.

Vibecoding is how I team up with AI to go from a reference to working code. Four flows, four sources of truth, and every result below is a single prompt with no iterations or fine-tuning.

The recipe

The skill isn't writing a 100-word prompt. It's knowing what to show the AI.

  1. 01A reference

    An image from Dribbble, a design system, a live product's code, or a Figma file.

  2. +
  3. 02A brief

    A design-brief.md: what to build, what goes in it, the dos and don'ts.

  4. →
  5. 03One prompt

    The AI reads both and writes working HTML or Vue in one go.
